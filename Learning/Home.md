# Regions and Societies: World Domination Patch

Compatibility patch: [World Domination 2.0](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680501610) bases, turrets and travelers are classified, populated and owned under [Regions and Societies](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060) territory rules.

**Current release:** v0.1.0 (RimWorld 1.6)

- **[Player's Guide](Players_Guide)** — what the patch does in-game and how to verify it is working. No settings; installed means enabled.
- **[Developer's Guide](Developers_Guide)** — the typed adapter, its classification table, and how to extend or override it through the Regions and Societies adapter registry.
