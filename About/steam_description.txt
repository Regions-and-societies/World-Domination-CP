[h1]Regions and Societies: World Domination Patch[/h1]
Compatibility patch: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680501610]World Domination 2.0[/url] bases, turrets and travelers are classified and owned under [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060]Regions and Societies[/url] territory rules.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Typed world object sorting:[/b] travelers (raids, drop pods, road builders, purchase parties) count as caravans; WD outposts hold ground as outposts.
[*] [b]Turrets hold ground:[/b] automated turrets classify as military installations that hold territory rather than being flattened into generic outposts.
[*] [b]Ruins release territory:[/b] a destroyed World Domination settlement becomes scenery - it stops claiming its region instead of holding it forever.
[*] [b]World Domination stays authoritative:[/b] the patch classifies WD objects but never resizes them - WD's own settlement grades, upgrades and outpost mechanics are untouched.
[*] [b]Both editions supported:[/b] works with the standard Regions and Societies or the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666526]Realistic Planets 2 edition[/url], with no false missing-dependency warning.
[/list]

The patch binds World Domination's assembly directly, so type drift is a compile error rather than a silent zero in-game. No settings of its own - installed means enabled.
